ChoiceLoop
An Autonomous AI Growth Agent for the Age of AI-Driven Decisions

The Big Idea

Don't just optimize for being found. Optimize for being chosen.

Traditional AI search optimization tools focus primarily on visibility: whether a business appears
in AI-generated recommendations and how highly it ranks.

But being recommended doesn't necessarily mean being chosen.

What if, instead of simply tracking search rankings, we could simulate how different customers
discover, compare, evaluate, and ultimately choose a restaurant?

ChoiceLoop is an AI-powered growth system that simulates diverse customer decision journeys,
identifies why a business loses potential customers, generates targeted improvements, and
tests whether those improvements make a difference.

Rather than treating AI visibility as a static ranking problem, we approach it as a dynamic
customer experience problem.

How It Works: Five Agents, One Continuous Loop

01. Persona Architect — Generate Customer Scenarios

Create a diverse set of simulated customers, including tourists, locals, couples, families, and
business travelers.

Each scenario combines factors such as location, budget, dining preferences, occasion, and
specific constraints.

The goal isn't simply to generate 100 personalities, but to explore a wide range of realistic
decision-making situations.

02. Customer Journey Simulator — Recreate the Decision Process

Each simulated customer moves through four stages:

   ●​   Discovery: Which restaurants does the AI recommend?
   ●​   Consideration: Which options make the shortlist?
   ●​   Decision: Which restaurant is selected, and why?
   ●​   Booking: Can the customer reach a viable reservation path?
The system records recommendations, comparisons, decisions, and potential drop-off points
without making actual reservations.

03. Diagnostic Analyst — Identify Why Customers Are Lost

Analyze the collected journeys to uncover specific weaknesses.

For example:

   ●​ Not discovered → AI search visibility problem
   ●​ Discovered but not selected → Missing information or weaker competitive positioning
   ●​ Selected but unable to proceed → Booking experience or UX problem

The agent compares the target restaurant with real competitors and uses verifiable public
information to identify evidence-based opportunities for improvement.

04. Experience Optimizer — Generate Targeted Solutions

Based on the diagnosed problems, the system generates practical improvements, such as
website copy, FAQs, menu descriptions, structured data, or recommendations for improving the
booking experience.

Proposed changes go through a human approval step before publication or implementation.

05. Measurement & Validation — Test, Learn, Repeat

The system reruns the same scenarios under controlled conditions and compares the results
before and after the proposed improvements.

If the results don't improve, the system returns to diagnosis and generates a revised solution.

This creates a continuous feedback loop rather than a one-time optimization report.

What Makes It Different?

Traditional tools measure how AI sees a business. ChoiceLoop investigates why a
customer chooses—or rejects—it.

Our approach goes beyond search visibility to understand where and why potential customers
are lost throughout the decision journey.

Instead of producing generic SEO recommendations, ChoiceLoop connects specific customer
segments, decision barriers, and actionable improvements.

Its central innovation is a repeatable system for diagnosing and testing the customer
experience.
Hackathon MVP

We'll build the prototype around Café Athena, a Mediterranean restaurant in Pacific Beach, San
Diego.

The MVP will include:

   ●​   20–30 diverse customer scenarios, with a framework that can scale to 100+
   ●​   Real AI search observations and competitor analysis
   ●​   Customer journey simulations and decision logs
   ●​   Evidence-based identification of conversion barriers
   ●​   Automatically generated improvement assets
   ●​   A before-and-after comparison dashboard

To maintain credibility, we'll distinguish between two types of evidence:

Live AI Search: Measures actual changes in AI search mentions and rankings.

Controlled Simulation: Tests how proposed improvements influence simulated customer
decisions under comparable conditions.

Simulated improvements will be presented as experimental findings, not proof of real-world
ranking or revenue growth.

Our Vision

As AI increasingly influences where people eat, shop, and spend, businesses need AI
that understands how those decisions are made.

ChoiceLoop aims to provide that intelligence.

We're not just building an AI search optimization tool.

We're building an AI system that helps businesses understand why they lose customers,
improve the experience, and continuously test how to become the better choice.
